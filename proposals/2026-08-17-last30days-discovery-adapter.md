# Proposal: evaluate `last30days` as an optional discovery adapter

Status: backlog

## Why retain the idea

The [`last30days` skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill) is designed
to search recent web and social discussion across several platforms. That may
help surface early user-practice signals that ordinary web research misses.

## Why not install it now

Beacon needs slow, cross-centre, source-graded learning as well as current
attention signals. A recency and engagement-oriented collector could over-weight
what is loud, English-language, platform-visible, or newly released. Its output
would be discovery material, never evidence status or validation.

## Small evaluation when the pulse archive is established

For two weekly pulses, run one identical, bounded question through direct Beacon
research and the optional tool. Compare:

- unique useful leads, duplicates, and unsupported assertions;
- US / EU / China and language coverage;
- source traceability and cost of later evidence grading;
- whether the tool changes what Beacon notices without making the weekly pulse
  too large.

Adopt it only if the comparison shows a repeatable discovery benefit. Keep all
Beacon grading, lifecycle, and append-only ledger rules unchanged.
