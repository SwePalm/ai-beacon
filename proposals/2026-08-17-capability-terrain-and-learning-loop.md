# Approved instrument update — capability terrain and learning loop

**Status:** approved by human review, 2026-08-17.  
**Decision:** update the Beacon instrument without importing the Attunement Observatory's forecasting cadence or theme architecture.

## Changes approved

1. Track early signals and later lifecycle reviews as separate append-only records.
2. Treat no material change as a valid outcome.
3. Carry prior calibration, unresolved signals, and open claims into selection.
4. Track conditional capability terrain across person, team, institution, and public loci; never present it as a fixed individual-skills syllabus.
5. Use small, evidence-backed, expiring calibration heuristics for recurring pipeline failures.

## Explicit non-changes

- Beacon remains condition-led, not a monthly 22-theme observatory.
- Evidence statuses are unchanged.
- Choice Briefs remain non-prescriptive and are still human-reviewed before publication.
