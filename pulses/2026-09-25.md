## Collection gaps

- The new study is about customer recovery but does not make a workplace's
  authority, workload, or downstream consequences visible.
- The US follow-up remains vendor-authored; the China implementation reference
  is marketing; no independent EU workflow trace was retained.
- Customer experience is better represented than the experience of workers who
  receive escalations, or people denied a meaningful route to a person.

## Next attention

In the October pulse series, seek one source that connects handoff initiation to
both sides of the transition: what the affected person experiences and what the
worker receives, can do, and is accountable for afterwards. Keep the new China
standard as a comparison lens, not evidence of compliance or adoption.
