# Beacon pulse — 2026-09-11

## Outcome

`watch`

The narrow watch found two bounded commercial accounts of agent-to-human handoff
design in the US and Europe. Both are published by delivery vendors or partners,
so they are useful discovery material rather than independent evidence of
outcomes. Together with the China-based Taobao field experiment, they support
continuing the handoff-and-recovery question; they do not justify a new full
pipeline run or a formal evidence upgrade.

## Collection gaps

- The new US and EU items are vendor/partner case studies, not independent or
  consented accounts from operators, reviewers, customers, or workers.
- The China item is attributable but too thin to answer the narrow watch.
- No item makes the handoff trigger, review materials, authority, time pressure,
  recovery result, or distribution of burden fully visible.
- EU AI Act implementation effects remain unobserved in a live workflow.

## Next attention

Seek one independent evaluation, regulator/worker account, or transparent
organisation case that documents the **same full chain**: delegated task →
handoff trigger → information supplied → authority and time to intervene →

and continue to keep the China lens open.
