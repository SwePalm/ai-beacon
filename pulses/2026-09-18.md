# Beacon pulse — 2026-09-18

## Outcome

`material change`

The change is bounded to customer-contact service in China: a current national
**recommended** standard now specifies how live and intelligent customer service
should allocate work, switch between systems, and carry information across the
handoff. It is not proof of implementation, legal enforcement, or a general
agent-work standard, but it is a concrete institutional articulation of the
handoff-and-recovery terrain Beacon has been watching.

## Due reviews and open claims

No formal lifecycle review is due; readiness hypotheses remain scheduled for
2026-11-16. The China standard is a new operating reference relevant to
`rh-2026-08-006` and `rh-2026-08-007`, but it does not corroborate either
hypothesis because adoption and lived effects are unobserved. No calibration
heuristic was due or added.

## Collection gaps

- The first concrete handoff reference is a national recommended standard, not
  a worker, customer, or organisation-level implementation trace.
- US survey evidence suggests delegation but does not reveal the replacement
  workflow or distribution of exception work.
- The EU gap persists, and no retained item describes people affected downstream
  by a failed handoff.
- The capture remains mostly English-language apart from the official China
  material; translation and source-type asymmetry need attention.

## Next attention

Use GB/T 47746—2026 as a bounded comparison lens, not a template: seek a real
workflow in any centre that reveals (1) allocation criteria, (2) a clear route
to a person, (3) information continuity, (4) authority and time to intervene,
and (5) the outcome after recovery. Check in November whether the standard has
produced attributable implementation evidence.
