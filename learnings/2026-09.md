# Beacon learning review — 2026-09

## Scope and record reviewed

This review compares the September pulses (4, 11, 18, and 25 September), the
August narrow-watch decision, the historical-baseline pilot, current readiness
hypotheses, and the capability-terrain framing. It is a learning record, not a
Choice Brief, a formal evidence promotion, or a change to the method.

## What changed, what held, and what remains open

- The 4 September pulse retained no material change. Product, policy, and
  executive announcements continued to be easier to find than attributable
  workflow evidence.
- The 11 September pulse found US and EU vendor/partner accounts and one China
  individual account. They made possible questions more concrete, but did not
  validate outcomes or reveal the full recovery chain.
- The 18 September pulse recorded a bounded material change: China’s current
  recommended customer-service standard provides an institutional vocabulary for
  allocation, transfer, and information continuity. It remains neither mandatory
  regulation nor evidence of uptake or lived effects. The accompanying US survey
  is a useful self-reported prevalence clue for delegation, not a workflow trace.
- The 25 September study made handoff initiation an empirical question: the
  route and timing by which a person re-enters a service interaction can affect
  recovery satisfaction. It does not resolve what workers receive, can decide,
  or carry afterwards.

The signals therefore converge only at the level of a **testable terrain**:
delegation creates an exception-and-recovery boundary, and that boundary has
conditions. They do not yet demonstrate a persistent, cross-setting change in
work, nor a basis for a prescriptive response.

## Capability terrain

| Locus | Emerging conditional capacity | What the September record does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Person / affected party | Recognise a breakdown, reach a person, and communicate enough context for the case to move forward. | Assuming every person has equal access, confidence, language capacity, or a meaningful route to a person. |
| Receiving worker | Interpret inherited material, ask targeted follow-up questions, and intervene with adequate time and authority. | Treating the worker as a frictionless backup or assuming a transcript itself makes a case actionable. |
| Team | Make allocation criteria, handoff triggers, context transfer, and recovery responsibility visible. | A universal workflow or a claim that more automation or more escalation is inherently better. |
| Institution / public system | Set contestable expectations for transfer and information continuity, then observe whether practice meets them. | Equating a recommended standard, a vendor configuration, or a product announcement with adoption or effective accountability. |

The main learning is methodological as much as substantive: Beacon should follow
the conditions around a handoff, not merely count handoffs or describe an AI
feature. This keeps the terrain portable across tools while respecting that its
conditions drift by setting.

## Signal lifecycle, claims, and calibration

No formal lifecycle decision is due before 2026-11-16. No record meets the
existing schema for formal promotion, so no ledger was appended.

- `rh-2026-08-006` and `rh-2026-08-007` are more precisely specified by the
  month’s records, but remain open rather than corroborated: evidence of human
  authority, time, and consequences is still missing.
- The vendor accounts remain discovery leads, not independent evidence. The
  China standard remains an operating reference, not an implementation claim.
- No calibration heuristic was due or added. The recurring source asymmetry is
  itself a calibration warning: vendor materials make a clean handoff legible;
  accounts from people who receive or depend on it do not.

## Collection gaps

- No retained source shows the whole chain in one attributable account:
  delegated task → handoff trigger → context received → authority and time →
  recovery or downstream consequence.
- US evidence lacks an independent, bounded workflow account; EU material is
  vendor-led; China evidence is a standard plus thin individual or commercial
  material.
- Customer experience is more visible than the receiving worker’s workload,
  discretion, compensation, and accountability.
- Customer-contact service dominates. There is no warrant to extend the pattern
  to 24/7 agents, knowledge work, or other domains without separate traces.
- English-language and provider-adjacent sources remain over-represented.

## Decision for October

`narrow watch`

Do not initiate a full pipeline run. The evidence is coherent enough to retain a
focused question, but not diverse or complete enough to claim a condition has
shifted. In October, collect one **paired transition trace** in a non-technical
US or EU setting if possible: an account that connects the affected person’s
experience to the receiving worker’s context, authority, time, and resulting
outcome. Keep the China standard as a non-prescriptive comparison lens and
continue looking for an attributable China implementation.

Escalate to a full run only if two or more independent, bounded accounts from
different settings make enough of that chain visible to support comparison. If
the same source imbalance persists through October, the next review should
consider adjusting collection rather than widening claims.
