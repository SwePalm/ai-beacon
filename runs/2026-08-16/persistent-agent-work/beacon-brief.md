# Beacon Choice Brief — persistent agent work

**Status:** draft live-condition pilot; not for publication.  
**Scope:** agentic delegation across US, EU, and China-centre signals; sources accessed 2026-08-16.

## 1. The condition

People and organisations are beginning to delegate multi-step work to agents that can continue across tools, files, sessions, and time. This is not a claim that a single model advance in November 2025 caused a universal shift, nor that agents replace human work.

## 2. The evidence terrain

OpenAI reports longer-horizon and parallel agent requests in sampled Codex data, including non-engineering use, but calls its task-horizon estimates directional ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-agents-are-transforming-work/)). Anthropic's November 2025 analysis distinguishes delegation from collaboration and shows a rise that later partially reversed, which complicates any straight-line narrative ([Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-primitives)).

OpenClaw's documentation offers managed gateway startup and identifies its Clawd predecessor as active from 25 November 2025; those are product and chronology observations, not adoption measures ([install](https://docs.openclaw.ai/install), [project chronology](https://docs.openclaw.ai/start/lore)). Anthropic documents audit events and manual/automatic approval information in Cowork ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-for-enterprise)).

The comparison is not US-only. Mistral describes durable, observable, approval-capable enterprise workflows in Europe ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/workflows/)); Alibaba describes an agentic enterprise platform available through DingTalk ([Alibaba](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-1971078136456019968)). An AP report offers one low-confidence account of student and internship use in China, not evidence of prevalence ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/0126a120113a92fa450ecb2e464b35bc)).

## 3. The historical rhyme

The closest rhyme is persistent workflow infrastructure, not generic job automation. It helps reveal the importance of queues, access, exceptions, and monitoring; it fails where agents can reinterpret goals and choose tools. See [history.md](history.md).

## 4. The human question

When a person delegates work that may continue while they are absent, who sets its boundaries; who sees what it did; who handles the exception; and who is answerable for the result?

## 5. The choice terrain

- **Learn:** How can people understand a task well enough to commission it, read its trace, and validate its result?
- **Protect:** What should not be silently delegated, accessed, remembered, sent, or decided without meaningful human review?
- **Redesign:** How can teams share context, handoffs, and exception work rather than turning them into invisible individual labour?
- **Contest:** Who may challenge an agent's action, a permission boundary, a metric, or an adverse result?
- **Wait:** Which uses should remain exploratory while reliability, ownership, and recourse are unclear?

These are possible response types, not advice.

## 6. Counter-case and uncertainty

Provider research and product documentation do not represent ordinary work. We lack independently observed workflows, user accounts outside product promotion, comparative data across the three centres, and evidence of who carries new coordination burdens. The Mac Mini and “agents 24/7” narrative remains a signal to investigate, not a present claim.

## 8. Open claims

1. **By 2026-11-16:** In a consented real workflow, what genuinely persists when an agent runs across time or tools, and what is still actively supervised by a person?
2. **By 2026-11-16:** Which validation, exception, and coordination duties are newly created—and for whom?
3. **By 2026-11-16:** Does persistent delegation broaden participation outside technical work, or concentrate capacity among people with money, time, permissions, and technical support?
4. **By 2026-11-16:** Do US–EU–China comparisons make the condition more legible without hiding affected people beyond those centres?
