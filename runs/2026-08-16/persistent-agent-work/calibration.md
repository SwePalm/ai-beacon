## Process learning

- November 2025 works as a start point for a trace, but not as a universal capability threshold.
- “Persistent delegation” is more precise than “agents work 24/7”: it includes continuity and boundary design without asserting continuous operation.
- The Mac Mini story is retained as an ungraded signal gap, not put into the evidence ledger.
