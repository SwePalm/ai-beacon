# Pattern synthesis — provisional baseline

## Bounded interpretation

**Interpretation:** When organisations automate bounded tasks, the observable outcome may depend as much on redesign, demand, location, and access to new roles as on the tool itself. Task substitution, expanded service, higher stated requirements, and local labour-market loss can all appear in the same broad family of technological change.

This is an interpretation of `ev-2026-08-002` through `ev-2026-08-006`; it is not a claim about a present AI condition.

## Who may benefit or bear burden

- People whose work expands into customer-facing, problem-solving, or coordinating tasks may gain discretion or opportunity, but the evidence does not establish that outcome for all workers.
- Workers whose entry tasks disappear or whose job requirements rise may face a narrower route in, especially where access to credentials or on-the-job learning is uneven.
- Communities concentrated in exposed industries may bear costs that an organisational or national view misses.
- Teams and institutions may inherit new verification, escalation, training, and accountability work even where throughput improves.

## Counter-case

An observed association between a tool and changed work may arise from concurrent reorganisation, trade, staffing decisions, or demand changes. The baseline cannot apportion those causes in a new setting.
