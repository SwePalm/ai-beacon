# Initial Theme Map

Themes are enduring questions, not a fixed taxonomy of AI topics. Use them to compare cases across time. Add or retire a theme only through a human-reviewed instrument proposal.

| ID | Theme | Standing question |
|---|---|---|
| agency | Agency and default | What choices become easier, harder, or invisible? |
| work | Work and effort | What changes in craft, entry paths, pace, and meaning? |
| judgment | Judgment and verification | Who is expected to assess what, and with what recourse? |
| attention | Attention and perception | What shapes what people see, believe, and have time to consider? |
| identity | Identity and representation | Who speaks, remembers, or acts in a person's name? |
| trust | Trust and relationship | What new dependencies, intimacies, and verification burdens emerge? |
| learning | Learning and becoming | What capacities are built, displaced, or newly needed? |
| power | Power and participation | Who sets defaults, bargains for protections, and can contest them? |
| care | Care and dignity | What becomes more supported, substituted, measured, or neglected? |
| public | Public life and institutions | What rules, public goods, and collective responses are changing? |
| creation | Creation and authorship | What is made, owned, credited, and experienced differently? |
| nature | Limits and the non-optimised | What remains beyond measurement, prediction, or utility? |
