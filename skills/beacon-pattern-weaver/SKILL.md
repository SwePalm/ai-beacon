# Beacon Pattern Weaver

Read graded evidence and historical comparison. Produce `pattern.md` and readiness-hypothesis records using `../../references/DATA_MODEL.md`.

Use conditional language. Name who may benefit, who may be burdened, plausible counter-cases, and whether the response belongs with a person, team, institution, or public system. Write `pattern.md` to the active run directory. Append readiness-hypothesis, pattern, and capability-terrain records to `state/*.jsonl` without changing historical lines, then run `scripts/validate-ledgers.sh`. A capability terrain record must be linked to the condition, name its locus, and say why it is not a universal personal-skill prescription. Offer `learn`, `protect`, `redesign`, `contest`, and `wait` as possible response types; do not decide among them for the reader.
